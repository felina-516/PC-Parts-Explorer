Motherboard

The motherboard is the large circuit board that links the computer's parts. It lets them share information and power.

What it does

It provides places to connect the CPU, RAM, storage, and other parts. Small pathways carry messages between them.

Where it goes

It is mounted flat against the inside of the computer case.
