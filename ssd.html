Fast storage

SSD

An SSD, or solid-state drive, stores your files and apps even when the computer is off. It uses memory chips and has no moving parts.

What it does

It loads files and starts programs quickly. It can also store your photos, games, and system files.

Where it goes

A small M.2 SSD slots directly into the motherboard, while other SSDs connect by a cable.
