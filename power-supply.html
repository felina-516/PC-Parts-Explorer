Powers the whole PC

Power Supply

The power supply unit, or PSU, takes electricity from the wall and makes it usable for the computer. It sends power to the other parts.

What it does

It provides steady electricity to the motherboard, drives, and graphics card. Its fan helps move warm air away.

Where it goes

It is fitted inside the computer case, usually at the bottom or back.
